## Setup boundary

Stackarr starts Tidarr, exposes its local or Portless link, preserves its durable configuration, and makes safe status and download actions available to the dashboard and trusted agents. Tidal authentication remains an interactive sign-in in Tidarr, and adding Tidarr as a Lidarr download client remains an explicit user step.
